# Code of Conduct

AlgoForge is a learning-oriented project. We want everyone — students, educators,
first-time contributors and experienced engineers — to feel welcome.

## Our standards

Expected behaviour:

- Be respectful and assume good intent; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and explain decisions; there are no stupid questions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Credit the work of others.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when
representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in the repository
profile, or via GitHub's "Report content" feature. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict or ban
participants whose behaviour violates this code.
